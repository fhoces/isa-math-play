# isa-math-play

Hands-on math for Isa (8). Lots of making and noticing, very little reading. A
grown-up reads the short notes; Isa does the doing.

The theme right now is **shapes, patterns, and symmetry**, the most visual,
playful corner of math, and it quietly connects to what Ben is doing. When Isa
folds a six-sided snowflake, that is the same hexagon Ben builds with a compass.
When her blocks fit together with no gaps, that is the start of what Ben calls
tiling.

## How to use it

Each `module-XX/` has three pieces:

- **`slides.html`** -- big, picture-driven slides to look at together at the
  screen.
- **`worksheet.pdf`** -- print it; this is the activity and the things to make.
- **`concepts.md`** -- the grown-up's notes: what the activity is teaching and
  the little wonder-questions to ask.

## How to run one

- Keep it to 20 to 30 minutes. Stop while it is still fun.
- Let Isa make a mess of tries. Wrong tries are how the noticing happens.
- The "Ask her" lines are little wonder-questions, not a quiz. A shrug is fine.

## Things worth owning

- A small **mirror** for symmetry play.
- A set of **pattern blocks** and a **tangram** (or use Mathigon Polypad, free).
