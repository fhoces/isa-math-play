isa-math-play -- learning plan

The scope for Isa's track. Playful, hands-on, very little reading. Each module is an activity with little wonder-questions, plus grown-up notes. Cross-links to Ben's geometry are noted because the same ideas rhyme across the two tracks.

Module 01 -- Mirror magic and fold-cut symmetry

  • Do: complete half-pictures with a standing mirror; fold-and-cut shapes (heart, snowflake); hunt for symmetry around the room.
  • Notice: a line of symmetry is a fold where both halves match; some shapes have one, some have many.
  • Cross-link: the six-fold snowflake is the same six as Ben's compass hexagon.

Module 02 -- Pattern blocks and tangrams

  • Do: fill a hexagon with smaller blocks (and count the ways); build bigger shapes and pictures; tile a surface with no gaps; solve tangram silhouettes.
  • Notice: small shapes build big ones; some shapes tile and some leave gaps (regular pentagons will not close up).
  • Cross-link: "no gaps" tiling is exactly Ben's tilings module; the pentagon gap is the same fact he proves with angles.

Module 03 -- Patterns and what comes next

  • Do: make a repeating pattern (beads, blocks, drawn shapes) and continue it; build a growing pattern (a staircase, the 1, 3, 6, 10 dots, or doubling) and predict the next step without building it.
  • Notice: a pattern has a rule; once you find the rule you can jump ahead. Repeating patterns repeat; growing patterns grow by a rule.
  • Cross-link: the growing 1, 3, 6, 10 staircase is the triangle numbers that Module 04 builds as real triangles.

Module 04 -- Number shapes you can build

  • Do: build square numbers (1, 4, 9, 16) as dot squares and triangle numbers (1, 3, 6, 10) as dot triangles; fit two copies of a triangle number together to make a rectangle.
  • Notice: numbers have shapes; a triangle number is half a rectangle; square numbers grow by the odd numbers (1, then +3, +5, +7).
  • Cross-link: square numbers echo Ben's square tiling and the pattern blocks of Module 02; the staircase is Module 03's growing pattern.

Module 05 -- A shape hunt

  • Do: hunt the house and outside for circles, triangles, rectangles, and hexagons; tally them; find the most symmetric thing; find a real tiling (a tile floor, a brick wall, a honeycomb).
  • Notice: shapes and symmetry are everywhere; this pulls the whole course together.
  • Cross-link: the honeycomb's hexagons are Module 01's snowflake six and Ben's compass hexagon; a brick wall is a tiling, the same "no gaps" idea as Module 02.

How hard / how long

Every module is a 20-to-30-minute sitting. Stop while it is still fun. There are no prerequisites; do them in any order, though 01 (symmetry) is the gentlest start.
